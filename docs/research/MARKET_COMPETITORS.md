# Market Research: Competitors and Landscape

Research into the current landscape of peer-to-peer (P2P) subscription sharing platforms reveals a growing industry, largely driven by the rising costs of digital subscriptions.

## Established Competitors

The current market contains several platforms attempting to solve the subscription fatigue problem:

1. **Sharesub (sharesub.com)**
   - **Model:** P2P account sharing platform.
   - **Features:** Over 700 services available. Hosts list available "slots" and prices. They offer an encrypted vault for credentials (AES 256) and manage automated recurring payments from co-subscribers.
   - **Fees:** The cost of the service is entirely free for the seller (all collected money goes to the bank account without hidden fees). They charge a commission to the buyer.

2. **Spliiit (spliiit.com)**
   - **Model:** The benchmark and leader in France.
   - **Features:** Regulated in France by the ACPR, guaranteeing secure payments and a secure login vault. Focuses heavily on multi-user subscriptions (like family plans).
   - **Reputation:** Known for robust security and legal compliance.

3. **GoSplit (gosplitgo.com)**
   - **Model:** "StreamPooling" – connecting subscription owners (Hosts) with renters (StreamPoolers).
   - **Features:** They generate unique passwords monthly to enhance security. Users change their password on the first of each month, and GoSplit distributes it to the renters. 

4. **ShareMySub (sharemysub.com)**
   - **Model:** P2P platform for splitting costs of Netflix, Spotify, Canva, Adobe, etc.
   - **Features:** Standard marketplace format (Host creates a listing, sets available slots, approves requests). Connecting people to split costs securely.

5. **GamsGo & Intermediary Platforms**
   - **Model:** Unlike P2P platforms, these platforms often bulk-purchase family plans or foreign subscriptions themselves and resell slots directly to users.
   - **Reputation:** Often face scrutiny over transparency and compliance with streaming services' Terms of Service. Access blockages are more frequently reported here.

## Key Takeaways for KeyShare

1. **The Market is Proven:** There is distinct, verified demand for this service. Users want to save money on subscriptions, and owners want to recoup costs.
2. **Security is Paramount:** Successful competitors provide "credential vaults" rather than just telling users to message each other passwords. Some (like GoSplit) even enforce monthly password rotation.
3. **Differentiation Opportunity:** Most existing platforms focus heavily on **Entertainment** (Netflix, Spotify). KeyShare can carve out a massive niche by focusing heavily on **B2B Software, AI Tools, and Educational Platforms** (like the WGU Udemy/Pluralsight example, Chegg, JetBrains), which are highly expensive and often underutilized.
