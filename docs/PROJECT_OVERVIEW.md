# KeyShare: Project Overview

## The Vision
KeyShare acts as a secure bridge (a peer-to-peer marketplace) between individuals who have access to underutilized resources (subscriptions, learning platforms, software licenses) and individuals who need temporary, affordable access to those same resources.

The modern digital landscape is filled with subscriptions, and many go unused. For example, universities (like WGU) provide their tech students with a plethora of free learning platforms, software licenses, and development tools. 

## The Core Problem
Many students or professionals use only a small fraction (e.g., 20%) of the resources available to them. The rest sit idle and go to waste. Meanwhile, someone else in the world desperately needs access to a specific platform (e.g., a specific Udemy course, JetBrains IDE, Pluralsight) but cannot afford the full retail price, especially if they only need it for a short duration.

## The Solution
KeyShare solves this inefficiency by creating a marketplace where:
- **Resource Owners (Sellers):** Can securely share access to their unused accounts or subscription slots. They are rewarded financially, offsetting their own costs or creating a passive income stream.
- **Resource Seekers (Buyers):** Can rent access to these resources at a fraction of the cost, gaining access to learning platforms, entertainment, or software tools when they need them.

## Example Use Cases
### 1. Educational & University Resources
WGU (and other universities) provide students with tools like:
- Udemy Business
- Pluralsight
- LinkedIn Learning
- Microsoft Azure (Dev Tools for Teaching)
- GitHub Student Developer Pack
- AWS Educate
- JetBrains, Axure, BaseCamp, Sketch, Autodesk

If a student isn't using their Udemy Business account, they can list it on KeyShare. Another user can pay a small monthly fee to use that student's account to take a specific course, creating a win-win.

### 2. Entertainment & Software
- **Streaming:** Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max
- **Software:** Adobe Creative Cloud, Canva Pro, Microsoft 365
- **AI Tools:** ChatGPT Plus, Midjourney, Claude Pro

## Our Role
KeyShare serves as the intermediary and escrow platform. We handle:
1. **Matchmaking:** Connecting the buyer and the seller.
2. **Payments:** Securely collecting payments from the buyer and releasing them to the seller (minus our platform fee).
3. **Credential Management:** Ensuring that passwords and access keys are shared securely without compromising the host's primary security.
